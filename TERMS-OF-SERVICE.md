## What this actually is today

Everything you can click on this site is real. The workspace's chat is a genuine, working UI —
what's simulated is the intelligence behind it (see [`HOW-IT-WORKS.md`](doc.html?doc=how-it-works)
Part three). No account is created by using this site. No data you type into the workspace preview
is sent anywhere — it's pattern-matched entirely in your own browser.

## When the real backend exists

These terms will need a real update once there's an actual harness making real changes to real
repos — this section is a placeholder for that, not a finished policy:

- You'll need to be on Christopher's invite allowlist to get a magic link at all.
- You bring your own Anthropic API key; usage is billed to you, not to Christopher.
- Harriot only touches the specific repo(s) you're invited to, only on a branch scoped to you,
  and never merges her own work — a human reviews every change before it's live.
- You're responsible for what you ask Harriot to do, the same way you'd be responsible for a
  change you asked any contractor to make on your behalf.

## What you shouldn't do with this (once it's real)

Ask Harriot to do anything illegal, ask her to touch a repo you weren't invited to, or try to use
the access she's given you to reach anything outside the scope you were granted. The access design
in [`ROADMAP.md`](doc.html?doc=roadmap) §4 exists specifically to make this hard to do by accident
and impossible to do quietly.
